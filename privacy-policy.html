Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2025  |  Effective: January 1, 2025

The Short Version

We don't collect your images. We don't sell your data. All image processing happens 100% locally in your browser. We use standard web analytics (with IP anonymization) and may display ads through Google AdSense.

1. Who We Are

GeminiClean ("we," "us," "our") is an independent web application available at geminiclean.online. We are not affiliated with Google LLC or any of its products. For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at privacy@geminiclean.online.

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Image Data

We collect zero image data. All image processing (upload, watermark detection, Reverse Alpha Blending, download) happens entirely within your browser using the Canvas API and Web Workers. Your images are never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

2.2 Usage Analytics

We may use web analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. This data is anonymized and includes:

  • Pages viewed and time on page
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Geographic region (country/city level, not precise location)
  • Referral source (how you found us)

IP addresses are anonymized before storage. We do not track individual users across sessions.

2.3 Contact Form Data

If you contact us via the contact form or email, we collect the information you voluntarily provide (name, email address, message content). This data is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is deleted after resolution.

2.4 Cookies

We use the following cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic site functionality (e.g., session management). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics cookies to understand site traffic patterns. You can opt out via your browser settings or the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense may use cookies to serve personalized ads. See Section 5 for details.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data to:

  • Operate and improve the GeminiClean tool
  • Respond to support inquiries
  • Analyze site usage to improve performance and usability
  • Display relevant advertisements to support free access to the tool

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Google Analytics — for anonymous usage statistics
  • Google AdSense — for ad serving (see Section 5)
  • Legal authorities — if required by applicable law

5. Google AdSense & Advertising

This website participates in Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. For more information, see Google's Advertising Policies.

6. Data Retention

Since we do not store image data, there is nothing to retain. Contact form data is retained for up to 90 days after resolution, then deleted. Analytics data is retained in anonymized form for up to 26 months.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to restriction — request limitation of how we process your data
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@geminiclean.online. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

GeminiClean is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

9. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests:

  • Email: privacy@geminiclean.online
  • Contact Form: Visit our Contact page
