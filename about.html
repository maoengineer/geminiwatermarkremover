About GeminiClean

A free, open, privacy-first tool for removing Google Gemini AI watermarks using mathematically precise Reverse Alpha Blending — not AI guessing.

Our Mission

Built on Transparency & Mathematical Precision

GeminiClean was created to fill a clear gap: users generating images with Google Gemini needed a reliable, trustworthy way to remove the visible watermark for legitimate personal use cases — portfolio presentation, creative projects, and design workflows.

Most "AI watermark removers" use generative inpainting — literally hallucinating new pixels to paint over the watermark region. GeminiClean does the opposite: it uses the exact mathematical inverse of the watermarking process to recover the original pixels with zero information loss.

Privacy First

Your images never touch our servers. Processing is 100% local.

Math, Not Magic

Deterministic, reproducible results based on algebra, not neural networks.

Free Forever

No paywalls, no credits, no subscriptions. Always free.
